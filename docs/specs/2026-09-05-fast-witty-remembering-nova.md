# fast-witty-remembering-nova

> Reachy Nova now answers within about a second of what you say instead of after a long silence, speaks like a friend with a dry wit rather than an eager helper, varies how it reacts to the same touch or face, hands the deeper reactions to Nova 2 Lite, and remembers who you are and what you talked about across restarts and session rotations.
> instruction: Verify on the robot: run a 10-minute conversation, grep the harness journal for the timing lines named in h-conditions on c2/c6/c12, and record Ori's verdict on tone in the delivery doc.

## Audience

- Ori and whoever is in the room with the robot — family and guests who talk to Nova and pet it; secondarily the operator reading the harness journal to see what the mind did and why.

## Before → After

- Before: Today every reply is preceded by 6–10 s of silence because the whole utterance is buffered before playback (journal 2026-09-05: 9.9 s before a 12.5 s reply, 6.0 s before a 4.2 s reply); the register is an eager assistant; every cue produces the same one-line reaction; a cue during speech is dropped; and every stream restart (including the documented ~8-minute rotation) wipes the conversation and adds a 3 s hole.
- After: You finish a sentence and Nova is already answering about a second later, in amy's voice, with a dry line rather than an offer to help; a pat while it is mid-sentence still gets a reaction the moment it stops talking; the same pat at breakfast and at night gets different reactions; the deeper reactions are shaped in the background by Nova 2 Lite; and after a restart or a stream rotation Nova still knows what you talked about today and what mattered.

## Why it matters

- A companion that answers ten seconds late and talks like a help desk is not a companion; the point of a robot in the room is that talking to it feels like talking to someone who is present, has a personality, and remembers the morning.

## Non-goals

- No direct ALSA streaming from the harness: reachy-mini-cli docs/operating-reachy.md measured (2026-08-02) that the XVF3800 AEC cancels only the daemon's own playback (2.06x baseline for the robot's own voice vs 4.72x peak for anything else played into the same speaker), so audio the harness pushes straight at the `reachymini_audio_sink` ALSA device would come back through the mic as an echo Sonic hears. Every playback path in this round goes through the daemon.
- No changes to the Kiro writer, the forge pipeline or the runtime's rules engine in this round; a runtime rule's say field stays a single fixed line (reachy-mini-cli reachy/behavior/rules.py caps it at 500 characters, no variants), so reaction variety is the mind's job, not the body's.
- No new Sonic tools: the action vocabulary (`run_behavior`, goto, `lock_face`, `recall_senses`, `stay_silent`, ...) is unchanged. What changes is the persona text, the inject context (mood, memory, Lite reaction plans), the playback timing and the session lifecycle.

## Assumptions

- A small mood variable lives in the harness (fed by pats, recognised faces, conversation turns and silence; decaying over minutes) and is rendered into the context Sonic and Lite see ('you are in a playful mood', 'nobody has spoken to you for ten minutes'), so the same pat reads differently across the day. This ports the idea of config/emotions.yaml, not the legacy emotions.py module, and emits nothing new on the cognition feed.
- A Lite reaction plan is delivered through exactly the same rendering as a template inject: the entry's voice marker (silent/brief) and the timed-quiet marker are appended to the plan text, the per-key dedupe reservation happens before the Lite call (so a duplicate pat never costs a second Lite call), the delivered text is what SenseHistory records, and voice: none cues never go to Lite at all.
- A proactive rotation waits for an idle moment — Sonic in listening state, no tool call in flight, speaker queue empty — with a hard deadline shortly before the 8-minute limit, so a reply, a tool result or a playing chunk is never cut by our own rotation; and the replayed history ends with a compact USER-role context block ('earlier today we talked about ...') plus the last two or three exchanges verbatim, capped in size, after which Nova says nothing until spoken to — a rotation must not produce a greeting.
- A deferred cue is delivered with its age in the text ('(someone petted you a few seconds ago, while you were talking)'), because with chunked playback Sonic finishes generating before the human has finished hearing, so a reaction to a mid-reply pat is already a beat late by the time it plays.

## Decisions

- Persona register: dry and teasing — in the spirit of Wit (Hoid) from Sanderson's Stormlight Archive: quick wordplay, mock the pretentious not the vulnerable, sudden unexpected sincerity, never cruel.
- Voice: amy (Nova 2 Sonic en-GB). English-only voice — matthew/tiffany remain the only polyglot options if other languages are ever wanted.
- Playback path: chunked playback through the daemon HTTP route ships now as the interim; the streaming speaker-feed seam (reachy-mini-cli#162) is the follow-up, not part of this round.
- Memory policy: Nova keeps the topics mentioned and the items judged important; importance is judged in the background by Nova 2 Lite, never on the voice path; everything expires after one day.
- Raw ledger policy: truncated to the last 24 hours at each compaction; nothing is written to the ledger (transcripts or senses) while a timed quiet is armed.
